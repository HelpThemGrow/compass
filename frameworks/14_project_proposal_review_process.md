## 1. Purpose and Scope

As Vibha grows and works across different states, domains and modes of
operation, more teams are proposing new ideas, projects, and renewing
existing ones. This framework gives everyone a shared, comparable standard
for pitching and reviewing project proposals, so good ideas move faster and
renewals are judged on evidence, not habit. It covers two tracks - **New
Initiative** and **Renewal** - reviewed at the same gate, against the same
rubric discipline, but asking different questions: new value versus proven
value.

---

## 2. The Toolkit

Six documents make up the framework:

| # | Tool | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New Project Proposal Template | The written case for a new initiative. 15 sections. |
| 2 | Renewal Proposal Template | The written case for continuing an existing project. 15 sections. |
| 3 | New Initiative Pitch Deck | The live pitch version of the New proposal. 17 slides. |
| 4 | Renewal Pitch Deck | The live pitch version of the Renewal proposal. 17 slides. |
| 5 | Partner Due Diligence & Capacity Assessment | Vetting a partner before a partnership-mode proposal goes forward. See **Partner Due Diligence Policy**. |
| 6 | Leadership & Board Scoring Rubric | How reviewers score and log every proposal reviewed, both tracks. |

---

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Programme Team** | Completes the Proposal Template and Pitch Deck. Presents live to Leadership, Board and Full Team. |
| **MEAL** | Completes the Partner Due Diligence & Capacity Assessment wherever a partnership is involved - see **Partner Due Diligence Policy, Section 0 - Application**. |
| **Leadership & Board** | Attends the live pitch and asks questions. Scores each proposal using the Rubric. Makes the gate decision: Go, Conditional Go, Hold, or Not Approved / Sunset. |
| **Full Vibha Team** | Attends the live pitch, wherever relevant. Can share a view even without a formal vote. Helps proposals reflect a Vibha-wide perspective. |

If a partner is involved in either track, the Partner Due Diligence &
Capacity Assessment feeds the Implementation Mode section of the Proposal
Template before the proposal reaches the gate.

---

## 4. The Process: Five Steps, Two Tracks

1. **Complete the Proposal.** New Project Proposal Template (New track) or
   Renewal Proposal Template (Renewal track) - 15 sections each. If a
   partner is involved, the Partner Due Diligence & Capacity Assessment is
   completed by MEAL alongside it.
2. **Build the Pitch.** New Initiative Pitch Deck or Renewal Pitch Deck -
   17 slides each. The Pitch Deck's Prep Checklist maps every slide back to
   a Proposal Template section, so nothing in the pitch is new - it is the
   written case boiled down for a live audience.
3. **Present and Decide, Live.** Presented to Leadership, Board and Full
   Team in a plain-language arc that ends on the "Let's Decide" slide with
   visible options. The Full Team can weigh in even without a formal vote.
4. **Score and Log.** Each proposal is scored on the Leadership & Board
   Scoring Rubric, against the criteria set matching its track. Scores are
   recorded on a per-proposal sheet and logged in the Session Portfolio Log
   - one row per proposal reviewed in that sitting.
5. **Gate Decision.** Go / Conditional Go / Hold / Not Approved (Renewal:
   Sunset). The Session Portfolio Log feeds the organisation's portfolio
   tracker.

## 5. New Initiative Track: Section Structure

The New Project Proposal Template's 15 sections, in four phases:

| Phase | Objective | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| **Orient** | Ground the reader before any case is made. | 1. Executive Summary · 2. Strategic Fit · 3. Implementation Mode · 4. Context |
| **Build the Case** | Justify the need and the chosen approach with evidence. | 5. Situation and Needs · 6. Theory of Change · 7. Design · 8. Stakeholder Fit · 9. Feasibility |
| **Prove It's Fundable** | Show the idea is measurable, costed, and manageable. | 10. MEAL Plan · 11. Budget · 12. Risk and Safeguarding · 13. Scale / Exit Criteria |
| **Make the Ask** | State exactly what's being asked for, with proof attached. | 14. Decision Requested · 15. Annexures |

The pitch deck follows the same shape in a shorter arc: Ground the Room
(Title, The Situation, The Opportunity, Alignment, The Idea) - Build
Confidence (Why This Approach, How It Works, Our Approach, Delivery,
Feasibility) - Show the Value (Outcomes and Impact, What It Will Cost,
Risks We're Watching) - Close (What We're Asking For, Let's Decide, Thank
You).

## 6. Renewal Track: Section Structure

The Renewal Proposal Template's 15 sections, in four phases:

| Phase | Objective | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| **Orient & Re-justify** | Re-earn relevance: nothing here is inherited. | 1. Executive Summary · 2. Context · 3. Strategic Fit · 4. Implementation Mode · 5. Continued Relevance |
| **The Track Record** | Prove past performance honestly, including what didn't work. | 6. Outcomes and Impact · 7. Challenges and Lessons · 8. MEAL Evidence · 9. Efficiency · 10. Risk Update |
| **The Forward Plan** | State what's actually being proposed next, and the honest call. | 11. Proposed Plan for Next Cycle · 12. Budget · 13. Team's Own Recommendation |
| **Make the Ask** | State exactly what's being asked for, with proof attached. | 14. Decision Requested · 15. Annexures |

The pitch deck mirrors this: Ground the Room (Title, Where We Started,
Alignment, How We're Delivering It, The Original Plan) - The Track Record
(What We Achieved, What We Learned, Is It Still Needed) - The Forward Ask
(What's Next, What Stays / Changes, Success Next Cycle, Investment,
Recommendation) - Close (What We're Asking For, Let's Decide, Thank You).

## 7. Scoring: The Leadership & Board Rubric

Scored at the Proposal Review gate, 1-5 per criterion, against the
criteria set matching the proposal's track - not both.

**New track** (8 criteria):

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Strategic fit with Educate / Empower / Enable | High |
| Needs evidence - quality of situation analysis | High |
| Programme logic - causal coherence of the Theory of Change | High |
| Feasibility and pilot design realism | Medium |
| Differentiation / innovation value | Medium |
| Resourcing realism | Medium |
| Partnership readiness (due diligence and capacity assessment complete, if applicable) | High |
| Risk and safeguarding readiness | High |

**Renewal track** (9 criteria):

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Evidence of outcomes and impact achieved (vs sunk cost) | High |
| Continued relevance to current need / context | High |
| Honesty in naming challenges, not just adaptations | Medium |
| Resource efficiency | Medium |
| Clarity and coherence of the proposed plan for next cycle | High |
| Quality and honesty of the team's self-assessment | High |
| Resourcing realism for next cycle | Medium |
| Partnership readiness (due diligence and capacity assessment current, if applicable) | High |
| Risk and safeguarding readiness | High |

High-weight criteria carry the most influence on the gate decision.

## 8. Gate Decisions and the Session Portfolio Log

Every reviewed proposal gets a composite record: total weighted score, any
risk flags raised, the gate decision, any conditions attached, and the next
review date - signed off by whoever chaired the session, with the date.

Gate decisions: **Go** · **Conditional Go** · **Hold** · **Not Approved /
Sunset**.

The **Session Portfolio Log** records, per review sitting: the review
session date, reviewers present, and how many proposals were on the
agenda - then one row per proposal reviewed (project name, track, score,
decision, notes). This log is what carries a single review sitting's
outcomes into the organisation's portfolio tracker.
