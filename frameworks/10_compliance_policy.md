## 1. Scope and Responsibility

This policy sets the statutory compliance standard we require of ourselves and of every funded partner and sub-grantee. Compliance is a **condition precedent** to disbursement: a lapsed registration or an unfiled statutory return suspends the next tranche under **Financial Policy, Section 6 — Disbursement**.

Responsibilities:

- Each partner names a **Compliance Officer** in writing at grant signing, and a deputy.
- The partner submits a **compliance declaration** with each annual report, listing every registration with its number and validity date, and every statutory return with its filing date and acknowledgement number.
- Our Partnerships Analyst verifies registrations against the Income Tax, MCA and FCRA public portals at due diligence and at every annual refresh (**Partner Due Diligence Policy, Section 1 — Legal Standing**).
- A **compliance calendar** for the financial year, listing every due date below, is maintained by each partner and reviewed at the quarterly joint review (**Programme Management SOP, Section 4 — Operating Systems**).
- Any notice, penalty, show-cause or inspection from any authority must be reported to us within **7 days** of receipt.

The financial year is **1 April to 31 March**. Dates below are statutory due dates as they stand at Version 1.0; the Compliance Officer must confirm current dates each year, as extensions and amendments are frequent.

## 2. Statutory Obligations

### 2.1 Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (FCRA)

Applies where the partner receives foreign contribution.

| Obligation | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| FCRA registration or prior permission | Valid certificate; registration valid **5 years** | Renewal application at least **6 months** before expiry |
| Designated FCRA account | **SBI, New Delhi Main Branch**, sole account for receipt of foreign contribution | Before first receipt |
| Utilisation account | May be opened in any scheduled bank; funds moved only from the designated account | Ongoing |
| Annual return **Form FC-4** with audited receipts and payments, income and expenditure, and balance sheet | Filed online | **31 December** each year, for the year ended 31 March |
| Quarterly disclosure of receipts on the partner's website | Donor, amount, date | Within **15 days** of the end of each quarter |
| Annual disclosure of FC accounts on the website | Audited statements | By **31 December** |
| Administrative expenditure from foreign contribution | Capped at **20%** of foreign contribution received in the year, per FCRA Section 8 | Ongoing |
| Sub-granting of foreign contribution | **Prohibited** — foreign contribution may not be transferred to any other person or organisation | Ongoing |
| Change of key office bearers, name, address or objects | Intimation in the prescribed form | Within **45 days** of change |

Foreign contribution must never be commingled with domestic funds. Cancellation, suspension or expiry of FCRA registration is an **automatic disqualification** for any grant routed as foreign contribution and must be reported to us immediately.

### 2.2 Income Tax: 12A/12AB and 80G

| Obligation | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **12A/12AB** registration | Valid certificate; validity **5 years** | Renewal application at least **6 months** before expiry |
| **80G** registration | Valid certificate; validity **5 years** | Renewal at least **6 months** before expiry |
| **Form 10BD** — statement of donations received | Filed by the donee | **31 May** following the financial year |
| **Form 10BE** — certificate of donation issued to each donor | Downloaded and issued | **31 May** |
| **Form 10B / 10BB** — audit report of the trust or institution | 10B where income exceeds INR 5 crore, or where foreign contribution is received, or where income is applied outside India; otherwise 10BB | At least **one month before the ITR due date**, i.e. by **30 September** |
| **ITR-7** income tax return | Filed annually | **31 October** (where audit applies) |
| **Form 9A / Form 10** — accumulation or deemed application options | Filed before the ITR due date | By **31 October**, and at least 2 months before ITR filing where required |
| Application of income | At least **85% of income** applied to charitable objects in the year, or accumulated under Form 10 | Within the financial year |

### 2.3 Corporate Social Responsibility: CSR-1

| Obligation | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **Form CSR-1** registration with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs | Mandatory before receiving CSR funds; generates a CSR Registration Number (CSRN) | Before first CSR receipt |
| Eligibility | Registered under Section 8, or a registered trust or society, with valid 12A and 80G, and **at least 3 years** of track record in similar activity | Ongoing |
| Utilisation certificate and impact report to the funding company | Per the CSR agreement | Within **90 days** of financial year end, unless the agreement specifies earlier |
| Unspent CSR funds | Returned to the company or transferred as directed so the company can meet its own transfer obligations | As directed, and in any case within **30 days** of financial year end |

### 2.4 POSH — Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013

| Obligation | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Internal Committee (IC) | Mandatory where **10 or more employees**; presiding officer a senior woman, at least half the members women, one external member | Before employing the tenth person |
| Written POSH policy, displayed at the workplace | Board approved | Ongoing |
| Awareness training for employees; capacity building for IC members | At least **once a year** | Annual |
| Complaint filing window | **90 days** from the incident, extendable by a further 90 days by the IC | Per complaint |
| Inquiry completion | Within **90 days** of the complaint | Per complaint |
| IC report to the employer | Within **10 days** of completing the inquiry | Per complaint |
| Employer action on recommendations | Within **60 days** | Per complaint |
| **Annual report to the District Officer** | Number of complaints received, disposed and pending | **31 January** each year |

See **Safeguarding Policy, Section 5 — Incident Reporting** for the parallel child-protection timelines.

### 2.5 Provident Fund and ESI

| Obligation | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **EPF** registration | Mandatory at **20 or more employees** | Within 30 days of reaching the threshold |
| EPF contribution deposit and **ECR** filing | 12% employee and 12% employer on wages | **15th of the following month** |
| **ESI** registration | Mandatory at **10 or more employees** (20 in some states); applies to employees earning up to **INR 21,000** per month | Within 15 days of applicability |
| ESI contribution deposit | 0.75% employee, 3.25% employer | **15th of the following month** |
| ESI half-yearly returns | For periods April–September and October–March | **11 November** and **11 May** |
| Professional tax, gratuity, minimum wages | As per state law | Per state schedule |

Employer statutory costs must be shown in the project budget (**Financial Policy, Section 2 — Budget Format**); omitting them understates the true cost of delivery.

### 2.6 Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)

| Obligation | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| TAN registration | Mandatory before deducting | Before first deduction |
| Deposit of TDS deducted | Challan 281 | **7th of the following month**; for March, **30 April** |
| Quarterly TDS returns (24Q salary, 26Q non-salary) | Filed online | **31 July**, **31 October**, **31 January**, **31 May** |
| TDS certificates: **Form 16** (salary) | Issued to employees | **15 June** |
| TDS certificates: **Form 16A** (non-salary) | Issued to deductees | Within **15 days** of the return due date |
| Common thresholds | Contractors (194C): INR 30,000 single / INR 1,00,000 aggregate; professional fees (194J): INR 30,000; rent (194I): INR 2,40,000 per year | Ongoing |
| Cash payment limit | No single cash payment above **INR 10,000** (Section 40A(3)) | Ongoing |

Late deposit attracts interest of **1% per month** (deducted but not deposited: **1.5% per month**); late return filing attracts a fee of **INR 200 per day**.

### 2.7 Annual Returns and Entity Filings

| Entity type | Filing | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Section 8 Company | **AOC-4** (financial statements) | Within **30 days** of the AGM |
| Section 8 Company | **MGT-7 / MGT-7A** (annual return) | Within **60 days** of the AGM |
| Section 8 Company | AGM held | By **30 September** |
| Section 8 Company | **DIR-3 KYC** for every director | **30 September** |
| Society | Annual return and audited accounts to the Registrar of Societies | Per state Act, commonly within **90 days** of the AGM or by 31 October |
| Trust | Filing with the Charity Commissioner or state authority, where applicable | Per state Act |
| All entities | **GST** returns where registered: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B | **11th** and **20th** of the following month |
| All entities | Statutory audit completed | By **30 September** |

### 2.8 Programme-Level Registrations

- Written government permission, NOC or MoU before school-based activity (**Government Engagement Protocol, Section 2 — Permissions**).
- Data-sharing agreement before receiving any government-held child data (**Data Protection Policy, Section 4**).
- Compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023** for all personal data, and with **POCSO** reporting duties for all child-facing work.
- Shops and Establishments registration for each office, per state law.

## 3. Monitoring and Consequences

- Compliance status is reviewed at **every quarterly joint review** and formally at each annual refresh.
- A **lapsed registration** (12A, 80G, FCRA, CSR-1) suspends disbursement immediately until restored.
- An **unfiled statutory return** beyond **30 days** past its due date must be reported to us with a remediation plan; beyond **90 days** it is escalated to the Grants Committee.
- Any penalty or prosecution is reported within **7 days** and recorded in the risk register under category **E3** (**Risk Management SOP, Section 3 — Contextual Risk**).
- Persistent non-compliance — **two or more lapses in one financial year** — is grounds for termination of the grant and exclusion from future funding for **three years**.
