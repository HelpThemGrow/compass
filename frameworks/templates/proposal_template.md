# New Project Proposal Template

**Document ID:** VIB-TPL-01
**Version:** 2.0
**Based on:** the 15-section structure named in Vibha_Proposal_final.pptx
and the per-section cues in Vibha_New_Initiative_Pitch_Deck.pptx (D:\PM360\,
2026-09-07)

The actual New Project Proposal Template file was not among the source
documents supplied - this outline reconstructs its 15 sections and what
each should contain from the toolkit overview deck and the pitch deck's
"Prep Checklist" (which maps every pitch slide back to a proposal section).
Programme staff should replace this outline with the real template's
section text once that file is available.

Complete every section; if one does not apply, say why rather than deleting
it.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The problem, the approach, the geography, who is presenting, and the
region(s) and date.

## 2. Strategic Fit

How this project supports Vibha's current strategic direction, vision and
mission - and which portfolio it sits in under Educate / Empower / Enable.

## 3. Implementation Mode

Direct implementation, or partnership. If a partner is involved: name them,
and state whether the Partner Due Diligence & Capacity Assessment is
complete or underway - see **Partner Due Diligence Policy**.

## 4. Context

Name the problem or need plainly - who is affected, where, and how the team
knows.

## 5. Situation and Needs

What the evidence shows: a named source - assessment, survey, or field
data - with a number if available. Where this operates, and Vibha's
existing presence there, if any.

## 6. Theory of Change

The logic in plain words, no jargon: what resources and effort go in, what
the team does, what gets delivered, what changes for beneficiaries, and the
bigger difference it adds up to.

## 7. Design

The approach in 3-4 design phases, summarised.

## 8. Stakeholder Fit

How the project will be delivered: directly by the Vibha team, with a
technical partner advising, or through a partner organisation running
activities on the ground. If a partner delivers any part of it, name them.

## 9. Feasibility

The pilot's scope, duration and scale - what is being tested before going
further - and the specific, measurable evidence that would trigger a
decision to scale up, hold, or stop.

## 10. MEAL Plan

Outcomes and impact the project is aiming for: for each, the level
(outcome or impact), what's being aimed for, how it will be known, and by
when. Outcomes are the direct change; impact is the bigger difference it
adds up to.

## 11. Budget

The line items and the total amount requested for this pilot or cycle.

## 12. Risk and Safeguarding

The top risks being watched (not the full register) and how each will be
managed. If this involves direct contact with children or vulnerable
groups, name the safeguarding checkpoint.

## 13. Scale / Exit Criteria

What scaling this up would require if the pilot succeeds, and the
conditions under which the project would end or hand over.

## 14. Decision Requested

What's being asked for: approval to proceed (as a pilot or full rollout),
budget sign-off (an amount), and any other enabling decision - a calendar
slot, staffing, or a channel.

## 15. Annexures

Supporting attachments: the Partner Due Diligence & Capacity Assessment (if
a partner is involved), and any other evidence referenced in the proposal.
