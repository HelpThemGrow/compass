# Renewal Proposal Template

**Document ID:** VIB-TPL-02
**Version:** 2.0
**Based on:** the 15-section structure named in Vibha_Proposal_final.pptx
(D:\PM360\, 2026-09-07)

The actual Renewal Proposal Template file was not among the source
documents supplied - this outline reconstructs its 15 sections from the
toolkit overview deck's Renewal Track breakdown. Programme staff should
replace this outline with the real template's section text once that file
is available.

Complete every section; if one does not apply, say why rather than deleting
it.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

What the previous cycle achieved against target, what changes in the next
cycle, and the amount and period requested.

## 2. Context

Where this project started - recall why it exists before asking to
continue it.

## 3. Strategic Fit

How the project continues to support Vibha's strategic direction, vision
and mission.

## 4. Implementation Mode

How the project is currently being delivered - directly, or in
partnership. If a partner is involved, confirm the Partner Due Diligence &
Capacity Assessment has been refreshed for this renewal - see
**Partner Due Diligence Policy**.

## 5. Continued Relevance

Whether the need this project addresses still exists in the current
context - re-justified, not assumed to carry over from the original
approval.

## 6. Outcomes and Impact

What was achieved: outcomes and impact against the previous cycle's
targets, with the evidence behind the claim.

## 7. Challenges and Lessons

What was learned - named honestly, including what did not work, not only
the adaptations made in response.

## 8. MEAL Evidence

The assessment data and evidence behind the outcomes reported in Section 6.

## 9. Efficiency

How efficiently resources were used against what was delivered - cost per
outcome, utilisation against budget, or an equivalent measure.

## 10. Risk Update

The risk register, updated: what materialised during the period, and any
new risks for the next cycle.

## 11. Proposed Plan for Next Cycle

What's next: what stays, what changes, and the success criteria for the
next cycle.

## 12. Budget

The renewal budget - line items and the total amount requested for the next
cycle, and the investment case for it.

## 13. Team's Own Recommendation

The team's own honest recommendation on whether and how to continue,
distinct from the request itself.

## 14. Decision Requested

What's being asked for: renewal approval, budget sign-off (an amount), and
any other enabling decision.

## 15. Annexures

Supporting attachments: the refreshed Partner Due Diligence & Capacity
Assessment (if a partner is involved), and any other evidence referenced in
the proposal.
