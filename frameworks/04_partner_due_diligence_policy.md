# Partner Due Diligence Policy

**Document ID:** VIB-FW-04
**Version:** 2.0
**Based on:** Vibha_Partner_Due_Diligence_Assessment.docx (D:\PM360\, 2026-09-07)
**Completed by:** MEAL

---

This document describes Vibha's real Partner Due Diligence Assessment tool -
the structured assessment of a prospective or renewing partner
organisation's legitimacy, governance, and compliance standing. It replaces
an earlier illustrative version of this policy that specified numeric
thresholds (minimum years of operation, an overhead percentage ceiling,
board size minimums, and so on) which do not appear in Vibha's actual
assessment tool. The source tool asks MEAL to record findings and exercise
judgement against each area below; it does not itself fix universal
numeric pass/fail thresholds. Where a specific figure is needed for a given
partner or grant size, that is a judgement call for MEAL and Leadership to
make and document at the time, not a fixed rule reproduced here.

This is the formal assessment referenced by the Implementation Mode section
of both the New Initiative and Renewal Proposal Templates. It is completed
by MEAL, not the proposing Programme Team, and is attached as an annexure
to any proposal that involves a partnership implementation mode. A new
partner is assessed in full; an existing partner's assessment is a
**Periodic Refresh** ahead of a renewal.

---

## 0. Application

Recorded for every assessment: partner organisation name, assessment type
(New Partner - first assessment, or Periodic Refresh - renewing an existing
partner), the related proposal or project this assessment supports, who at
MEAL conducted it, the assessment date, and the next review due date
(typically 12-24 months from the assessment, or before the next renewal -
whichever comes first).

---

## 1. Organisational Identity and Registration

Establishes who the partner legally is.

- **Legal name**, registration type (Trust / Society / Section 8 Company),
  registration number and date, registering authority, and years in
  operation.
- **Document status check**, for each of: Registration certificate, PAN,
  12A certificate, 80G certificate, and FCRA certificate (where the partner
  handles foreign funds). For each, MEAL records status, the
  reference number or expiry date, and any notes.

---

## 2. Governance and Leadership

Establishes whether the partner is well-governed, not just legally
constituted.

- Board size and composition - number of members, diversity of skills and
  background.
- Board tenure pattern - stable, regularly refreshed, or long-unchanged.
- Whether conflict-of-interest declarations are on file for all board
  members (Yes / No / Partial).
- Leadership stability - Executive Director / CEO tenure, and any recent
  key management turnover.
- Whether minutes of the last three board meetings were reviewed, and
  whether that review raised any governance concerns.

---

## 3. Financial Health and Management

Establishes whether the partner can be trusted to hold and account for
Vibha's funds.

- **Audited financials reviewed**, recorded per financial year: auditor
  name, whether the audit opinion was qualified, total income (INR), and
  total expenditure (INR).
- **Financial management systems**: the accounting software or method in
  use, internal controls, and segregation of duties - noting anything that
  falls short of what a grant of this size would require.
- **Financial red flags**: irregular reserves, going-concern language in
  the audit, or unexplained gaps between years' figures.

---

## 4. Track Record and References

Establishes whether the partner has actually delivered comparable work.

- **Prior grant / partnership experience**: funder or partner name, size
  and duration of each engagement, and its outcome.
- **Independent reference check**: referee name and organisation, their
  relationship to the partner, and a summary of their feedback. **At least
  one independent reference is expected before due diligence can be marked
  complete.**
- Any complaints, disputes, or terminated partnerships on record.

---

## 5. Legal, Regulatory and Reputational Standing

- Active litigation or regulatory action - none identified, or details.
- Whether a media or regulatory adverse-findings search was conducted, and
  its findings if any.
- Whether sanctions or watchlist screening was conducted, and its findings
  if any.

---

## 6. Safeguarding and Ethics

- Whether a child protection / safeguarding policy is in place, and if so,
  its date.
- Whether a code of conduct / anti-fraud policy is in place, and its date.
- Whether a whistleblower or grievance mechanism is in place.
- Whether staff safeguarding training is on record (Yes / No / Partial).

---

## 7. Mission and Values Alignment

Whether the partner's mission, values and current activities align with
Vibha's, noting any conflicting engagements - political, proselytising, or
work in conflict with Vibha's child-safeguarding or education mandate.

---

## 8. Overall Due Diligence Determination

Every assessment closes with:

- **Risk level**: Low / Medium / High.
- **Determination**: Cleared to proceed / Cleared with conditions / Not
  cleared.
- **Conditions** attached, if any.
- **Next review due** date.
- Sign-off: assessed by (MEAL), with date; reviewed / countersigned by,
  with date.

A partnership-mode proposal cannot proceed to the Proposal Review gate
without a determination recorded here - see
**Project Proposal Review Process, Section 3 - If a Partner Is Involved**.
