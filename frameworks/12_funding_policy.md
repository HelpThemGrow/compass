## 1. What We Fund and What We Do Not Fund

### 1.1 What We Fund

We fund work that raises measured learning levels in government schools, consistent with **Strategy Note, Section 1 — Mission and Scope**. Fundable work falls in one of the three portfolios in **Strategy Note, Section 2 — Portfolio Structure**:

- **Educate** — FLN for Grades 1–5; remedial and catch-up learning for Grades 3–8; structured reading and mathematics programmes; materials and libraries tied to a curriculum and an assessment.
- **Empower** — teacher and head-teacher development through the government cadre; academic mentoring by CRC, BRC and DIET personnel; SMC and parent engagement tied to a learning indicator; adolescent learning programmes.
- **Enable** — assessment design, learning-data systems, academic supervision reform, technical assistance to SCERT, DIET and Samagra Shiksha, and evidence generation informing state policy.

We fund the **full cost of delivery**, including project staff, MEL (**3–7% of project cost**) and administrative overhead up to the ceiling in **Financial Policy, Section 4 — Overhead Limits**.

### 1.2 What We Do Not Fund

The following are refused, normally at concept-note stage:

1. **Private, low-fee-private or unaided schools.** Our work is in government and government-aided schools only.
2. **Individual child sponsorship**, scholarships to named individuals, or bursaries.
3. **Standalone infrastructure**: school buildings, toilets, boundary walls, land or building purchase. Land and building purchase is refused absolutely; other capital may not exceed **10% of grant value** and must be instrumental to a learning outcome.
4. **Projects with no learning indicator** — enrolment, attendance, health, nutrition or sports projects where learning is assumed rather than measured.
5. **Religious activity, religious instruction or proselytisation of any kind.**
6. **Political activity**, party affiliation, election campaigning or lobbying for a political party.
7. **Endowments, corpus contributions, loan repayment or deficit financing.**
8. **Retrospective costs** — expenditure incurred before the grant agreement date.
9. **Cash payments, honoraria, gifts or salary top-ups to serving government employees.** Reimbursement of actual travel and per-diem at government rates is permitted (**Government Engagement Protocol, Section 0**).
10. **Research with no implementation pathway**, conferences, or study tours as standalone activities.
11. **Emergency relief and disaster response.**
12. **Re-granting to an unnamed third party**, and any onward transfer of foreign contribution (prohibited by FCRA, see **Compliance Policy, Section 2.1**).
13. **Organisations that fail due diligence** under **Partner Due Diligence Policy**, including any partner with a cancelled or suspended FCRA registration, an undisclosed safeguarding incident, or an unremediated unsatisfactory financial control rating.

Exceptions to items 1, 3 and 10 require **written Board approval** with reasons minuted. There are no exceptions to items 5, 6, 9 and 12.

## 2. Grant Sizes and Durations

These four tiers are used identically across **Partner Due Diligence Policy, Section 0**, **MEL Framework, Section 6 — Verification**, **Financial Policy, Section 6 — Disbursement** and **Strategy Note, Section 4**.

| Tier | Grant size | Typical purpose | Maximum duration | Typical reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Under **INR 10 lakh** | Pilot, feasibility, capability grant | **12 months** | Up to 2,000 children / 20 schools |
| **Tier 2** | **INR 10–50 lakh** | Proof of concept | **24 months** | 2,000–10,000 children / 20–100 schools |
| **Tier 3** | **INR 50 lakh – 2 crore** | Block or district demonstration | **36 months** | 10,000–40,000 children / 100–500 schools |
| **Tier 4** | **Above INR 2 crore** | District, multi-district or state scale-up and institutionalisation | **48 months**, extendable to **60 months** for institutionalisation | 40,000+ children / 500+ schools |

Rules:

- Grants of **12 months or less** are made only for pilots, bridge funding or capability grants. Anything expected to change learning levels should run at least **24 months**.
- **Multi-year grants** are approved for the full period but disbursed annually against performance (**Financial Policy, Section 6**).
- **Pilot grants** (Tier 1, evidence bar not yet met per **Strategy Note, Section 3**) are capped at **INR 25 lakh over 12 months** and, in aggregate, at **10% of the annual grant budget**.
- **Concentration limits**: no partner above **15%** of the annual grant budget; no state above **35%**.
- **Total continuous funding** to one partner for one model is capped at **five years** (**Government Engagement Protocol, Section 5 — Exit**).
- A project may advance **one stage per funding cycle** on the pathway in **Strategy Note, Section 4**.

## 3. Additionality

We fund what would not otherwise happen. Every proposal must make the additionality argument explicitly.

**Required disclosures**: all other funders of this project and of the organisation, with amounts, periods and restrictions, covering the last two years and the proposed grant period (**Partner Due Diligence Policy, Section 6 — Disclosures**); any overlap with another submitted or funded proposal, with the percentage of overlap; and government funding available for the same activity under Samagra Shiksha, the state FLN mission or the district AWP&B, with why it is not sufficient.

**Tests applied**

1. **No duplication of government provision.** Where the state already funds the activity, we fund only the incremental element — quality assurance, capability, assessment, technical assistance — and this must be specified.
2. **No double funding.** The same cost may not be claimed from two funders; the proposal carries a signed statement to that effect.
3. **Displacement test.** Our grant must not substitute for funding the partner would have received anyway. Where comparable funding is secured, we fund the extension, not the base.
4. **Concentration test.** Our grant must not exceed **50%** of the partner's total annual expenditure for a new partner, or **70%** for an existing partner in an institutionalisation phase (**Partner Due Diligence Policy, Section 3 — Financial Health**).
5. **Government cost-share.** For Stage 4 and Stage 5 projects, a government cash or in-kind contribution of at least **25%** of total project cost is expected and must be valued in the budget.
6. **Leverage.** Co-funding is welcomed and reported, but may not be counted toward our disbursement.

Failure to disclose other funding, discovered after approval, is a **material breach** of the grant agreement.

## 4. Approval Thresholds and Workflow

### 4.1 Approval Authority

| Grant size | Approving authority | Additional requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Under **INR 10 lakh** (Tier 1) | **Director of Programmes**, under delegated authority | Reported to the Grants Committee at its next meeting |
| **INR 10–50 lakh** (Tier 2) | **Grants Committee** | Simple majority; quorum of 3 |
| **INR 50 lakh – 2 crore** (Tier 3) | **Board of Trustees**, on the recommendation of the Grants Committee | Independent evaluation budgeted (**MEL Framework, Section 6**) |
| **Above INR 2 crore** (Tier 4) | **Board of Trustees**, by a **two-thirds majority** | External technical review of the design before the Board paper is tabled |

Further authorities:

- **Overhead exception** above 15% and up to 20%: **Board approval in writing**, minuted (**Financial Policy, Section 4**).
- **Budget variance** above 20% on a line, or any movement into or out of overhead: Grants Committee (**Financial Policy, Section 7.2**).
- **Cost per child** more than 25% above benchmark: Grants Committee; more than 50% above: Board (**Financial Policy, Section 3**).
- **Exception to the "government schools only" rule**, up to 10% of project schools: Grants Committee.
- **Grant termination** or fund recovery: Director of Programmes with the Director of Finance, reported to the Board within **14 days**.
- **No-cost extension** up to 6 months: Director of Programmes; beyond that, Grants Committee.

Any Board or Committee member with a relationship to the applicant must declare it and **recuse themselves** from discussion and vote; the recusal is minuted.

### 4.2 Workflow and Timelines

| Stage | Owner | Target time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Concept note submitted | Applicant | — |
| 2. Screening against Section 1 and mission fit | Programme manager | **15 working days** to a decision |
| 3. Full proposal invited, using the standard proposal template | Applicant | Applicant given **30 days** to submit |
| 4. Completeness check against the template | Programme manager | **5 working days** |
| 5. Technical appraisal against **Programme Design Standards** and **MEL Framework** | Programme manager and MEL lead | **20 working days** |
| 6. Due diligence per **Partner Due Diligence Policy** | Partnerships Analyst and Finance | **20 working days**, running in parallel |
| 7. Field or reference visit | Programme manager | Within the appraisal window |
| 8. Clarifications to the applicant | Programme manager | Applicant given **10 working days** to respond |
| 9. Appraisal note and recommendation prepared | Programme manager | **10 working days** |
| 10. Grants Committee or Board decision | As per Section 4.1 | At the next scheduled meeting; Grants Committee meets **monthly**, the Board **quarterly** |
| 11. Decision communicated in writing | Head of Partnerships | **10 working days** from the decision |
| 12. Grant agreement signed | Both parties | **30 days** from the decision |
| 13. First tranche released | Finance | **15 working days** from a fully signed agreement (**Financial Policy, Section 6**) |

Total elapsed time from full proposal to first disbursement should not exceed **120 days**. Applicants are informed of the expected decision date at Stage 3.

**Decisions**: approve; approve with conditions (conditions stated in writing, with a compliance date); defer for clarification (once only); or decline with written reasons. Declined applicants may reapply after **12 months**, or earlier if the stated reasons have been addressed. Renewal applications follow **Renewal Policy** and must be submitted at least **90 days** before the current grant ends.
