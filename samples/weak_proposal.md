## Problem Statement

The condition of education in rural areas of the state is very poor. Thousands of children are studying in government schools but they are not learning anything. The teachers are not regular, the infrastructure is broken, the parents are illiterate and the children have no motivation. Many children drop out after primary classes and start working in the fields or migrate with their parents.

Girls are especially affected. They are made to stay at home and look after their younger siblings. Because of this they lose interest in studies and finally leave the school. This is a very sad situation and something must be done about it urgently.

The government is running many schemes but they are not reaching the last child. There is a big gap between the policy and the ground reality. Our organisation has seen this gap very closely during our field work and we feel that only an NGO like ours, which has the trust of the community, can bridge this gap effectively.

Nobody has done any proper study in these areas so far. Whatever we are stating here is based on our direct observation and interaction with the community, which we believe is more reliable than any survey report.

## Project Design

The project will be implemented in the rural areas of the state, covering several villages and their surrounding hamlets. The exact list of villages will be finalised after the grant is sanctioned. We expect to cover a good number of schools and reach thousands of children.

The main activities of the project will be as follows:

**Learning centres.** We will open learning centres in the villages where children will come after school hours and study. Our teachers will teach them Hindi, English and Mathematics. The timing of the classes will be TBD depending on the convenience of the children.

**Distribution of materials.** We will distribute books, notebooks, pens, school bags and other materials to the needy children so that they do not face any difficulty in studying.

**Awareness programmes.** We will organise rallies, street plays and meetings in the villages to create awareness among the parents about the importance of education. This will motivate them to send their children to school regularly.

**Teacher support.** We will also work with the school teachers and help them in their work. The nature of this support will be decided in consultation with the school authorities.

**Special focus on girls.** Girls will be given special attention and encouragement so that they do not drop out.

The project duration will be one year, extendable depending on the availability of funds.
